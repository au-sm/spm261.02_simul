SPM261.02 Soccer League · you never know this week's opponent formation in advance — here's what you DO know

How To Use This

Every lineup is submitted independently the same week it's played, so you can never see an opponent's exact formation or strategy before your own match. Two things ARE public the whole season, though, and give a real basis for your weekly call without guessing:

Roster. Every team's drafted players, and their ATT/DEF, have been public since Draft Day. That alone tells you what formations are even realistic for an opponent, and roughly how their Attack and Defense compare to yours — no matter which exact formation they end up picking.

Game tape. Once a team has played, the formation and strategy they actually used that round is a matter of record below — scout their tendencies the way a real analyst studies past matches, not a guess about next week.

This page does not tell you what to play. Scouting Attack/Defense below is an estimate from each team's whole-roster averages under a neutral Balanced strategy — not their actual number any given week, since nobody's exact starting XI or formation is known ahead of time. Your formation, strategy, and the rationale behind them are still your call to make and defend.
